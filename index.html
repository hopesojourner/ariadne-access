AriadneAccess

AriadneAccess is a private connection for one person. It lets that person's own assistant software, running on their own computer, work with their own Google account.

What it does

  • Gmail: reads, searches, organizes and sends the owner's mail when the owner asks.
  • Google Calendar: reads the owner's calendars and adds, changes or removes events when the owner asks.
  • Google Drive: reads the owner's files, and creates or updates files the app itself has made.

Who can use it

Only its owner. AriadneAccess is not a public service and not a product. Nobody else can sign up for it, and it is not offered to other Google users.

Privacy

How Google data is handled is set out in the privacy policy.
