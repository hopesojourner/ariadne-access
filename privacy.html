AriadneAccess privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026.

Who this covers

AriadneAccess is used by one person, its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It has no other users.

What Google data it can access

With the owner's permission, AriadneAccess can access the following in the owner's Google account:

  • Gmail: messages and labels, to read, search, organize and send mail.
  • Google Calendar: calendars and events, to read them and to add, change or remove events.
  • Google Drive: files, to read them, and to create or update files the app itself has made.

How the data is used

Only to carry out the owner's own requests. Examples are reading and summarizing mail, sending a message the owner asked for, checking or adding a calendar event, and finding or saving a file.

The data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone else.

Where the data goes

AriadneAccess runs on the owner's own computer, and the sign-in it receives from Google is stored there.

When the owner asks the assistant to work with a message, an event or a file, that content may be processed by the AI model the owner has chosen for the assistant. That can be a model running on the owner's computer or an AI service the owner holds an account with.

Keeping and deleting

Content the assistant has worked with may be kept in the assistant's own notes and history on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it.

The owner can remove AriadneAccess's access at any time in the Google Account settings, under third-party apps and services.

This website

These pages collect nothing from visitors. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as visitors' IP addresses, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Contact

Questions go to the support address shown for AriadneAccess on Google's sign-in screen.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
